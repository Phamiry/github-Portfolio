## What I learned

I learned how AI can help me organize my resume by rearranging sections and improving the formatting. I also learned that I should review the changes myself to make sure everything is accurate and professional.

## Limitations and responsible use

AI can help improve my resume, but it may not always organize everything correctly or understand my personal experience. I made sure to review the changes, check that all my information was accurate, and only include skills and accomplishments that I actually have.
