Thank you for playing farkle

Controlls
To start the game press the Play button
After that you will have three buttons to choose from
Roll-rerolls the dice
Score-adds the score from the dice selected to the running score
End Turn-ends your turn and adds your running score to your total score

Selecting Dice
to select a die click on it. this will createa a yellow outline arround the die. this means it is selected.
Once selected if you use the score button the selected dice will be locked shown by a green boarder. Locked dice cannot be selected
only dice selected will be scored.

Scoring
to end your turn and add your running score to your final score you must have atleast 200 points in your running score

farkle no score ends turn without adding running score to total

Winning
After the first player reaches 10,000 points each player will have one turn to try achieve a higher score. After play returns to the first person to reach 10,000 points
the game is finished and the player with the most points wins.
