01 / MY BACKGROUND

About me

My name is Jaylon Pham, and I am a cybersecurity student at Lamar University with an expected graduation date of December 2026. I have earned a 3.93 GPA and been recognized on the President’s List, Dean’s List, and A Honor Roll. I am building my professional foundation in technology, security, and problem-solving. Cybersecurity interests me because it combines technical investigation with an important purpose, helping people and organizations protect systems, information, and the services they depend on.

As I prepare to enter the workforce, I want to demonstrate more than a list of course titles. I want to show how I approach a problem, explain my decisions, test my solutions, and communicate results clearly. This portfolio brings together some of my projects I have created, a working security-focused software demonstration, space for additional academic work, and documentation that explains the development process. The email triage assistant in this portfolio explores how a lightweight Python application can identify potentially suspicious messages and, when configured locally, consult a language model for additional analysis.

My coursework has covered Computer Networks, Operating Systems, Digital Forensics, Software Engineering, Cyber Security Systems, Cyber Security Networks, and Database Design. Alongside my studies, I work as a deckhand for a commercial crabberman, maintaining equipment and supporting safe, coordinated operations. I have earned the Google Cybersecurity Certificate and am working toward CompTIA Security+. I am especially interested in learning how security teams investigate alerts, assess risk, analyze unusual activity, and improve day-to-day defenses. I value careful documentation because effective technical work must be understandable to teammates, supervisors, and people who are not security specialists.

My near-term goal is to earn an opportunity in an entry-level cybersecurity, security operations, or IT role where I can develop practical experience and continue learning from experienced professionals. Over time, I hope to strengthen my skills in scripting, network security, incident response, and security tooling. I am using this portfolio to track that growth, share projects publicly, and make it easier for employers and internship recruiters to see what I can build and explain.

As I complete my degree, I plan to update this site with verified coursework, project evidence, new skills, and accomplishments. I am excited to contribute to a team that takes security, learning, and collaboration seriously.
